# CodeAlpha Power BI Internship Projects

This repository contains Power BI dashboards built as part of my Data Analytics internship with CodeAlpha.

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- Power BI Desktop
- Power Query (data cleaning and transformation)
- DAX (measures and calculated columns)

## 👥 Task 2: HR Analytics Dashboard
A 3-page dashboard analyzing employee attrition using the IBM HR Analytics dataset.

- **Page 1 – Overview:** KPI cards (Total Employees, Active Employees, Attrition Rate, Avg Monthly Income), attrition by department, employees by job role
- **Page 2 – Attrition Analysis:** Attrition rate by age group, monthly income vs attrition, department vs job role breakdown
- **Page 3 – Workforce Insights:** Years at company vs monthly income, average job satisfaction, education field distribution by department

📁 [`Task2_HR_Analytics/CodeAlpha_HRAnalytics.pbix`](./Task2_HR_Analytics/CodeAlpha_HRAnalytics.pbix)
📄 [`Task2_HR_Analytics/WA_Fn-UseC_-HR-Employee-Attrition.csv`](./Task2_HR_Analytics/WA_Fn-UseC_-HR-Employee-Attrition.csv)

## 🏠 Task 3: Real Estate Market Trends
A 3-page dashboard analyzing King County house sales data.

- **Page 1 – Market Overview:** KPI cards (Total Properties, Avg Property Price, Median Price, Avg Price per SqFt), price trends over time, properties by price category
- **Page 2 – Geographic Heatmap:** Interactive map showing property price distribution by zip code
- **Page 3 – Investment Insights:** Living area vs price scatter plot, average price by bedroom count, zipcode-level price matrix

📁 [`Task3_RealEstate/CodeAlpha_RealEstate.pbix`](./Task3_RealEstate/CodeAlpha_RealEstate.pbix)
📄 [`Task3_RealEstate/kc_house_data.csv`](./Task3_RealEstate/kc_house_data.csv)
